# Code Of Conduct

MyHR follows a simple standard: be respectful, constructive, and professional.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Give technical feedback based on evidence.
- Respect academic integrity and authorship.
- Handle HR-style data and research outputs responsibly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private data, credentials, or vulnerability details.
- Misrepresenting generated research outputs as validated scientific results.
- Removing attribution or claiming another contributor's work.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its academic integrity.
