## Paper Requirements

- Main text: 30 to 35 pages.
- The cover page, optional assignment, table of contents, bibliography, abstract, and appendices do not count toward the main-text limit.
- Figures and tables do count toward the main-text limit.
- A4 paper, single-column layout.
- Times New Roman or another readable serif font, 12 pt.
- Single line spacing.
- Margins between 1.5 cm and 3.5 cm on every side.
- Figure and table content must remain legible in print; labels below 10 pt should be avoided.
- The published thesis templates are starting points only and require adaptation to these TDK rules.
