# TDK Protocol Change Proposals

Status: **Pending supervisor approval. None of these proposals unlocks the holdout.**

## 1. Primary Confirmatory Question

- Population: rule-negative records from independently generated residual-error scenarios.
- Comparison: dimension-matched policy-aware versus raw Isolation Forest.
- Endpoint: paired scenario-level average-precision difference.
- Independent unit: scenario, not employee record.
- Inference: two-sided paired permutation test and 95% scenario-clustered bootstrap interval.
- Decision rule: the lower confidence bound must exceed zero.

## 2. Scenario Eligibility

Primary scenarios must be predeclared as residual causal mechanisms and contain positive rule-negative cases by administrative design, not detector optimization. Explicit rule-violation scenarios remain secondary. Scenarios with no residual positives cannot contribute a defined scenario-level AP and must not be silently counted as zero in the confirmatory endpoint.

## 3. Operational Capacity

Every deterministic finding is mandatory. ML may fill unused capacity up to a nominal 10% total budget. If rules exceed 10%, the actual floor rises to retain them. Report actual alert counts and constraint feasibility.

## 4. Secondary Multiplicity

Rare-legitimate rate, ordinary-clean rate, F1, recall, precision, false-positive rate, per-class recall, and utility analyses are secondary. Their tests are exploratory and receive Holm correction across the generated comparison family.

## 5. Policy-Version Context

Add policy version, jurisdiction, recognized service, and effective-date evidence before final scenario freeze. Current single-policy evaluation falsely classifies some lawful transition histories.

## 6. Threshold Deployment Study

Keep validation-F1 and target-FPR thresholds secondary. Compare organization-specific calibration against a global threshold before deployment claims. An infeasible target FPR must be reported, not forced.

## 7. Utility Interpretation

Charge review cost to every alert, missed-error cost to false negatives, and additional escalation harm to rare-legitimate false alerts. Report multiple cost profiles without declaring one correct.

## Decisions Required

1. Approve or revise the primary hypothesis and AP endpoint.
2. Decide whether deterministic findings are mandatory above nominal capacity.
3. Approve independent scenario cards and policy-version assumptions.
4. Approve the expert-review method and reviewers.
5. Decide whether authorized external calibration data are available.

Until these decisions are recorded, the protocol remains draft and the holdout remains locked.
