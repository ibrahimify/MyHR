# TDK Protocol Amendment: Expert Realism Review

Date: 2026-11-24
Status: **Approved before protocol freeze**

## Change

The planned 48-case blinded rating study was not executed. It is replaced for
this protocol version by a qualitative realism review reported by the author as
completed by two independent academic experts with complementary qualifications.
The reviewers authorized continued work and the protected holdout stage.

Reviewer identities are private. The one-time external authorization stores only
anonymous reviewer IDs, qualifications, evidence locators, and SHA-256 checksums.
No identity is stored in the repository or experiment artifact.

## Scientific consequence

The qualitative review supports face validity only. It does not produce or
support claims about:

- case-level realism ratings;
- label correctness;
- reviewer agreement or Cohen's kappa;
- adjudicated ground truth;
- anomaly prevalence; or
- transfer to real organisations.

The hypotheses, primary endpoint, secondary family, equal-capacity comparison,
feature families, scenario matrix, seeds, model family, and statistical analysis
are unchanged. This amendment therefore changes the strength of the realism
evidence, not the confirmatory comparison.

## Reporting rule

Future manuscripts must describe the review as qualitative expert review and
state its limitations. They must not describe the unexecuted 48-case protocol as
completed or report invented ratings, agreement, adjudication, or reviewer
identities.
